# Good Company

A wellness app designed to help people understand and manage everyday stress through simple habits, self-reflection, and practical tools.

Good Company is intended for general wellness, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Project Status

**Early development — Product definition**

The project is currently in its initial exploration stage. The MVP scope, technology stack, architecture, and target platforms are still being defined.

## Product Overview

### Goal

Create a simple, private, and supportive experience where people can track how they feel, recognize patterns over time, and explore practical ways to manage stress.

### Proposed MVP

The initial product may include:

- Daily stress and mood tracking.
- Optional notes about situations, activities, or contributing factors.
- Visual history to identify patterns over time.
- Short breathing, pause, and reflection exercises.
- Optional, configurable reminders.

These features are exploratory and subject to validation and prioritization.

### Product Principles

- **Privacy:** Collect only necessary information and communicate clearly how it is used.
- **Non-judgment:** Support reflection without labeling emotions or experiences as good or bad.
- **User control:** Allow users to manage their entries, data, and reminders.
- **Accessibility:** Design clear, inclusive, and usable experiences.
- **Simplicity:** Prioritize meaningful interactions over unnecessary complexity.
